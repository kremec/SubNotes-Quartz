- Commonly known work-life balance concept only exists when you are not enjoying your job
- "It makes sense doing only doing X hours of work" is wrong, it's all about motivation
- The choice about how much time you have for your craft will detrimetally determine how much you will excel at it - putting in the hours will make you better at it
- Being a olympic competitor takes serious dedication and work, you can't be an olympic programmer if you clock out at 5, and if you don't make that choice, you won't be one of the best programmers
- If you don't often get to the point where you can say "oh i want to continue doing this, but i can't" because of tiredness, you aren't working at your highest productivity level
- The cause of burnout is often that you are not living up to the expectations (of sucess, ...)

- BUT ... in life you can have different priorities than being the best programmer - family can/should come first
- Hard work in sense of pure hours should be something that happens because you want to work, not because you (think you) need to
