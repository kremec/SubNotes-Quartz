- When you wake up, your emotions haven't "started" yet - start doing the first thing on your agenda
- As you will do it, you'll likely get in "the flow", which will keep you doing it
- You will be tempted to get the dopamine shot from your phone - don't let yourself pick it up in your hand, rather just stay where you are doing nothing
- As you are doing nothing, the brain will try to force the next dopamine shot out of you, and if you don't let yourself get it from anything other than the next productive thing on your to-do list, you will eventually be forced to get up and start doing it
